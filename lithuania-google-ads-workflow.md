# Lithuania Google Ads workflow: Ergovent air diffusers

Observation date: 20 October 2026.

Purpose: grow high-quality clicks in Lithuania while holding spend flat. Use organic coverage for queries Ergovent already wins. Use paid search only where a click is new demand.

## What this pass can and cannot prove

A company is listed below only when a Lithuania filter (region code 2440) returned creatives on 20 October 2026. “Might share Ergovent keywords” is an inference from product overlap. It is not an observed bid. Confirm overlap in Google Ads Auction insights and the search terms report.

## Executive takeaway

The advertisers most likely to meet Ergovent on plaster-in diffuser queries are Ventmann and two Lithuanian shops, Proventas and Conrema Baltic (airway.lt). Ergovent already has 20 Lithuania creatives, 17 of them text. Extra budget belongs on a small set of generic product terms and a capped Ventmann ad group, not on the Ergovent brand.

## Top 3 strategic moves

1. Hold the brand and move any extra budget into generic product terms. Score 89/100. Brand queries are already Ergovent’s to defend. Scaling them raises spend on people who already found the brand.
2. Open a separate exact-match ad group for Ventmann names, with proof-led copy and a hard daily cap. Score 86/100. These searchers already know a rival, so the ad has to earn the click on Ergovent strengths.
3. Add negatives and a reseller check before any budget increase. Score 83/100. Airway lists a cheaper Ventmann Circle above Ergovent RONDO, and broad HVAC advertisers can soak up generic terms.

## Campaign structure

Three campaigns, or three ad groups inside one Lithuania Search campaign if the budget must stay in a single pot. Do not mix the keyword types.

### 1. Brand, exact match, capped

Keywords to keep and not to scale: ergovent, ergovent difuzorius, ergovent rondo, ergovent kvadro, ergovent lineo, ergovent lineo pro.

Rule: if Google Search Console shows position 1–3 and the paid click-through rate is already strong, bid only high enough to sit above a reseller. Do not add budget here.

### 2. Generic product, phrase and exact, this is where new clicks should come from

Split ad groups so the landing page matches the query.

- Round gypsum: priglaistomas difuzorius, gipsinis difuzorius, nematomas difuzorius, dažomas difuzorius, difuzorius rekuperacinei sistemai. Land on RONDO.
- Linear slot: plyšinis difuzorius, linijinis difuzorius, berėmis difuzorius, plyšinis priglaistomas difuzorius. Land on LINEO PRO.
- Comfort: coanda difuzorius, difuzorius su coanda efektu, tylus difuzorius. Land on RONDO COANDA.
- Specifier: difuzorius gipso kartono luboms, difuzorius architektams, bim difuzorius. Land on the downloads and BIM page.

### 3. Competitor names, exact match only, separate budget cap

Keywords: ventmann, ventmann difuzorius, ventmann circle, ventmann line, ventmann square.

Leave out Komfovent, SALDA, Zehnder, Systemair, Brink, Flexit and Vallox until a search term proves the person wants a diffuser rather than a heat-recovery unit.

Copy rules for this group:

- Do not name the other brand.
- Do not say “better than” or show a comparison table.
- Lead with a proof the searcher can check: airflow along the ceiling, Red Dot, iF Design Award 2025, damper included, paintable gypsum, made in Lithuania, BIM and a mounting guide.

## How more clicks can stay inside the same budget

1. Stop paying for queries organic already answers. Brand, and any generic term that holds position 1–3 with a strong click-through rate, stays on a low bid.
2. Buy exact and phrase match. Broad match on “difuzorius” will buy Vilpra-type HVAC traffic.
3. One landing page per ad group. A mismatched page lowers Quality Score and raises the cost of the same click.
4. A daily cap on the competitor group so a few expensive clicks cannot move the account cost.
5. Put guides (what a slot diffuser is, how to choose 100 or 125 mm, how Coanda works) on the website. Those informational queries stay organic.

## Weekly run, 45 minutes

Do this every Monday. Archive the sheet. Escalate only findings that score 80 or above.

1. Open Ads Transparency Centre with region Lithuania: https://adstransparency.google.com/?region=LT
2. Read each account in the fixed list: Ergovent UAB, Ventmann (EE, UAB, UK), MB Proventas, UAB Conrema Baltic, UAB NIT, Celsis, UAB Armo, Vilpra, Systemair, plus any new name from last week’s search terms.
3. Record advertiser, creative count, format mix, newest first-shown date, and any new headline. Quote the headline. Save the creative URL.
4. In Google Ads, export the last 7 days of search terms and Auction insights for the three groups.
5. Mark each term: already owned by organic, generic and worth buying, competitor name, or waste.
6. Add negatives the same day. Do not raise the account budget in the same week as a new keyword set.
7. Score new competitor creatives with the model in `competitor-monitoring-agent-concept.md`.
8. Publish the one-sentence takeaway, top 3 moves, action tracker and watchlist.
